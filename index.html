Brand design

A good brand is easy to recognise and easy to use. We start with your business and your customers, then design an identity your team can apply with confidence — on screen, in print and in new markets.

  • — Brand strategy, positioning and tone of voice
  • — Logo and visual identity

UI & UX design

We design the screens and flows of websites and apps before a line of code is written. You see clickable prototypes early. When you need it built, our app team at RUSKA NORDIC Labs takes it to launch.

  • — User research and user flows
  • — Wireframes and clickable prototypes
  • — UI design for iOS, Android and web
  • — Design systems and developer hand-off
  • — Websites designed and launched

Process // how we work

Four steps, about three weeks.

  1. STEP 01

    Discover

    A free intro call about your business, your users and what success looks like.

  2. STEP 02

    Define

    A short proposal with scope, timeline and a fixed price. Research and strategy before any visuals.

  3. STEP 03

    Design

    Concepts, then one direction refined together. Clickable prototypes for UI/UX work.

  4. STEP 04

    Deliver

    Final files, guidelines and hand-off — or launch with our team at RUSKA NORDIC Labs.

About // Kangasala, Finland

A small studio in Finland, working across cultures.

INNO Design Studio is a brand and UI/UX design studio based in Kangasala, Finland. We are a small team on purpose: every project is designed by the people you talk to.

  1. 2015

    Founded as a design and communications company, producing and localizing content for international clients in the Chinese market.

  2. 2016

    Began representing Nordic design in East Asia, bringing European lifestyle products to new customers.

  3. 2018

    Launched RUSKA NORDIC, our brand for mobile apps and fine porcelain trade.

  4. Today

    Focused on brand design and UI/UX design, with our own apps as a working lab for what we design for clients.
